G98 Notifications, Filed On Time

G98 is the easy one — which is exactly why it gets forgotten. It's a notification, not an application: you install, you commission, and you have 28 days to tell the network operator. Miss that window and the job is non-compliant.

What G98 covers

ENA Engineering Recommendation G98 governs micro-generation connecting at or below 16A per phase. In practice:

  • Single phase: up to roughly 3.68kW
  • Three phase: up to roughly 11.04kW

The threshold is measured on inverter output, not panel capacity. A 4.5kWp array on a 3.68kW inverter is a G98 job. A 3.6kWp array on a 5kW inverter is not — that's G99.

Add a battery and the assumption usually breaks. Storage counts as generation, and the combined inverter rating is what the operator assesses.

The 28 day window

G98 is connect and notify. You don't wait for permission and there is no assessment period to schedule around. You install, commission, and the notification has to reach the network operator within 28 days of commissioning.

That deadline is the whole risk profile of G98. Nothing blocks the install, so nothing forces the paperwork, and it slides. A notification filed late is a breach of the connection agreement, and for MCS-certified installers it's a compliance issue with your certification body as well.

What a G98 notification needs

Site and supply

  • Full install address and postcode
  • Import MPAN
  • Phase configuration
  • Any existing generation on site

Equipment and commissioning

  • Inverter manufacturer and model
  • Rated output in kW and A per phase
  • G98 type test certificate reference
  • Total array capacity in kWp
  • Commissioning date

The type test certificate reference is the item that most often bounces a G98. Inverter firmware revisions supersede certificates, so a reference that was valid last year may not be valid now.

The export MPAN comes after

Filing the notification isn't the end of it. The export MPAN follows separately, typically within a few weeks, and your customer can't register for the Smart Export Guarantee until it lands. Some operators issue an acceptance email instead, which the customer's supplier then uses to request the export MPAN.

Either way, someone has to watch for it. Until it arrives the customer is exporting for free, and that's usually when they ring you.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to wait for approval on a G98?

No. G98 is a notification, not an application. You install and commission, then notify the network operator within 28 days.

What happens if I miss the 28 day window?

The system is connected outside the terms of the connection agreement. File it late rather than not at all — but expect it to surface at property sale, supplier switch, or your next MCS audit.

Does a battery stay within G98?

Usually not. A battery counts as generation and the combined inverter rating typically pushes the system past 16A per phase, which makes it a G99 application instead.
